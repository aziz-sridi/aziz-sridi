# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People visiting Mohamed Aziz Sridi's GitHub profile: AI internship recruiters, collaborators, and fellow developers.

## Product Purpose

A personal GitHub README that presents Aziz as an AI and Machine Learning engineering student seeking an AI internship. Mobile apps and other software are hobbies. Give visitors a direct way to connect and keep the memorable Saitama-inspired personality.

## Brand Personality

Playful, understated, inventive. Preserve the existing ASCII Saitama and add a speaking bubble with animated text. The user chose One-Punch Commits as the contribution animation.

The contribution illustration uses GitHub's green calendar palette and the original frame-by-frame pixel-art Saitama sprite credited to Hacker93 and shared by SantinoP. Keep the existing punch and calendar scatter. Preserve the yellow-orange half-circle silhouette drawn from the user's supplied tapered white reference. The user explicitly rejected replacing this silhouette with simple stroked arcs. The smaller second wave should convey an aftershock and the punch's velocity and power through its motion: trail closely and fade sooner while keeping the chosen shape. Make the punch impact clearly visible: a filled burst, outward sparks, a short hold at contact, and brief screen shake before the waves travel across the calendar. Keep everything flat and 2D, with no elliptical hoops, perspective, gradients, blur, or 3D effects. Squares return to their exact dates, with month headings aligned to GitHub's Sunday week columns. Use the real idle and serious-punch frames rather than a simplified SVG character.

## Anti-references

Do not copy Pac-Man or add a generic contribution snake. The user likes the animated text in github.com/jkdevcode, but wants an original contribution animation. Keep centered LinkedIn and email links in place of section navigation.

## Design Principles

- Give the existing character a voice.
- Let useful profile content remain easy to scan.
- Lead with AI and machine learning; label app development as a hobby.
- Remove explanations of the artwork and generic profile filler.
- Keep the About section short, direct, and natural.
- Leave featured projects out until the user requests them.
- Make the contribution calendar the source of the animation.
- Show the complete AI, frontend, backend, database, mobile, and developer toolkit as a list with icons, without a table or collapsed disclosure.
- Keep contact links centered beneath the introduction, with email first. Email and LinkedIn use clickable icons with accessible labels and no visible text.
- Let the contribution card carry its own title; do not repeat it in a Markdown heading.
- Work inside GitHub README constraints with self-contained SVGs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use descriptive image alternatives and native headings and links. Honor reduced-motion preferences in SVGs and provide both light and dark artwork. No additional accessibility requirements were specified.
